Updating the Atelier Problem Scenario for the Summative Assignment

Formative feedback is feedback that is given during the learning process to help you
improve your understanding and performance.

The key formative tasks in this course supports the final development of your
Research Proposal. Carefully review your feedback on the Atelier Research Poster
- this feedback should directly inform how you tackle your summative Research Proposal submission.

As a formative task, the Atelier Research Poster is part of a cumulative learning
process; your Research Proposal must not be a stand alone theoretical exercise; it
must be rigorously synthesised from the evidence, fieldnotes, and critical reflections
generated during your coursework from Week 4 onwards.

Updating the Problem Scenario to align with the Atelier Research Proposal task:

We can use some hypothetical feedback provided by Ethnography Studio (link) to
a hypothetical applicant to generate a new problem scenario for the whole
cohort. The feedback is generic (whereas your own formative feedback will be very
specific), but it nevertheless applies to everyone:

"The Ethnography Studio (link) have responded positively to my Atelier Project Poster
submission. I am now one of several candidates in Round 2 of their selection process.
They have asked me to further develop plans for a fully-fledged 12 week Atelier
Research Project that will successfully integrate artistic methods with anthropology
'embracing the uncertainty and ambiguities that ethnographic research affords for
creative thinking.' I can nominate any [site] to work in as long as it a public (not
private) setting. I must now submit a formal Research Proposal for them to review. It
should support a 'system of action'. (Gell, 1998: 6)

The Ethnography Studio panel gave me feedback on my Research Poster. They noted
that it was weak in terms of artistic methods and that it did not yet fully support a
'system of action'. (Gell, 1998: 6) How can I take this feedback on board and create a
Research Proposal that will enhance understanding and innovation in each domain and
ensure that my project situates actions (or creates stituations)?"
